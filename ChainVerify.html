STAGE 01 • COLLECT THE EVIDENCE

Bring Everything Together

ChainVerify gathers the evidence that matters before anything is verified — invoices, browser activity, system data, webhooks, and other records.

Each item is captured with information about where it came from and when it was created, creating a reliable starting point for verification.

• invoices • browser activity • system data • webhooks • fiscal records
STAGE 02 • CHECK THE EVIDENCE

Inspect Every Detail

ChainVerify examines each piece of evidence to confirm that it is authentic and hasn't been changed.

It checks things like digital fingerprints, signatures, timestamps, and source information before allowing the evidence to move forward.

DIGITAL FINGERPRINTS SIGNATURES TIMESTAMPS SOURCE PROVENANCE
STAGE 03 • CROSS-CHECK THE RESULTS

Verify It From Multiple Sources

ChainVerify doesn't rely on a single check.

It compares evidence across different systems — such as AI activity, invoices and accounting records, browser evidence, and audit records — to make sure the information agrees. Those independent checks come together to create one trusted result.

AI Activity • VerifyAPI Invoices • InvoiceProof Audit Records • AuditProof Browser Evidence
STAGE 04 • CREATE THE PROOF

Turn Evidence Into Verifiable Proof

Once the evidence checks out, ChainVerify combines it into a permanent proof record.

The proof contains the information needed to show what was verified, where the evidence came from, and that it has not been altered.

✓ PROOF RECORD CREATED ✓ UNALTERED MERKLE ROOT ✓ EVIDENCE ANCHORED
STAGE 05 • CONNECT THE PROOF

Build a Trusted Chain of Evidence

Each verified proof becomes part of a connected record that can be checked again later.

That gives businesses, auditors, applications, and AI agents a clear way to answer: What happened? Where did the evidence come from? Was it verified? Can we prove it?
