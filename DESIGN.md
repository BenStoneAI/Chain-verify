## Overview

ChainVerify is a hybrid brand/product site for buyers who need evidence before trusting AI work or high-stakes workflow output. The visual system is anchored in an **infrastructure-grade technical verification dossier**: completely free of purple/violet clichés, engineered with high-contrast electric cyan, deep teal, and obsidian graphite.

## Colors (No Purple)

The shared environment uses:
- **Graphite Black `#0A0A0F`**: page and app canvas.
- **Raised Graphite `#11151F`**: primary cards and panels.
- **Deep Graphite `#171D29`**: elevated cards and proof records.
- **Soft White `#F8F9FC`**: headlines and primary text.
- **Cool Slate `#8F9DB2`**: secondary text and labels.

### Brand Identity Spectrum
- **Electric Cyan `#00E5FF`**: main accent, C highlight, primary commercial CTAs.
- **Bright Teal `#14B8A6`**: mid-gradient, hover states, VerifyAPI accent.
- **Deep Teal `#0D9488`**: gradient depth, secondary accents.
- **Ice Cyan `#67E8F9`**: true verified states and luminous highlights.
- **Emerald `#10B981`**: true confirmed/success states only.

### Brand Gradient
`linear-gradient(135deg, #00E5FF 0%, #14B8A6 55%, #0D9488 100%)`
Applied to:
1. The "V" form in the logo
2. The small hex-node chain trail
3. The word "Verify" in the wordmark
4. Primary CTAs and verified-state moments

### Semantic Product Accents
- **VerifyAPI**: Bright Teal `#14B8A6`
- **AuditProof**: Soft Cyan `#22D3EE`
- **InvoiceProof**: Warm Amber `#FFB45C`
